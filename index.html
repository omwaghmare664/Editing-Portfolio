About

One person, from first cut to final sound.

I’m Om, a video editor and motion designer based in India. Om Visuals is my personal practice, so the person you brief is the person who edits, animates and finishes your video.

My focus is SaaS and product video: showing clearly what software does. I care about rhythm, clip alignment, typography, speed ramps, sound design and music, the details that make a cut feel intentional.

Before this focus I edited advertisements, documentary-style content, music videos, gaming content and creator content. I’m now looking for product-led projects from SaaS founders, software companies, marketing teams and agencies that need dependable editing and motion support.

Client feedback

Before you write

Good questions.

How fast is turnaround?

Short-form edits ship in 48 hours. Long-form projects take 4 to 7 days.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included with every project.

How is a project priced?

Every project is quoted individually. The price depends on video length, animation complexity, number of scenes, UI animation, sound design, deliverables, revisions and deadline. Send the details through the form and I will reply with a quote or follow-up questions.

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief: what the product is, what the video should achieve, where it will be used, and your deadline. Screen recordings, footage, brand assets and reference videos help if you have them.

Which software do you use?

Adobe Premiere Pro for editing and Adobe After Effects for motion graphics.
